We are officially staying in Zimbabwe! (Long Term)
 
Hi family,
We decided it was important to interrupt our regularly scheduled newsletter to share some exciting news.
After months of prayer, seeking wisdom, and seeing countless ways God has confirmed His leading, we've made the decision to move to Zimbabwe and stay long term.
This decision didn't come quickly or lightly. It has been shaped through many answered prayers, open doors, conversations, and what feels like a million little confirmations from God along the way. Individually, God has been stirring our hearts with countless reasons to stay, and together He has given us a deep sense of peace that this is where He is calling us.
While we don't know exactly what the future holds, we are confident in the One who does. We are incredibly excited to continue serving at Karanda, investing in the people here, and seeing how God continues to work in the years ahead.
 
Why We’re Staying
We love the people
Relationships are at the heart of this culture, and it has been refreshing to live in a place where people genuinely value getting to know one another and caring for each other well. We have come to treasure the friendships we've built here. The maintenance team has become like family to us, and the kids never fail to make us laugh. It is a privilege to do life alongside such incredible people, and we are thankful for each relationship God has given us here.
 
 
We love our jobs
God has uniquely equipped both of us with engineering skills that complement one another and allow us to thrive in an environment where resources are limited and challenges are endless. Together, we have the opportunity to solve problems, improve critical systems, and serve in practical ways that help the hospital continue its ministry. It is incredible to see how God has prepared us individually, and as a team - for the work He has called us to here.
We love the opportunities to serve God
Serving at Karanda Mission Hospital allows us to be part of something so much greater than ourselves. By helping keep the hospital running, and sharing the hope of the Gospel along the way, we get to support a ministry that is impacting lives every single day. Through the hospital's medical care, community outreach, education, and Christ-centered ministry, people are experiencing both physical healing and the love of Jesus. It is a privilege to use the engineering skills God has given us to help make that possible.
