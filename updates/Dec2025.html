The Journey begins…



By God's hand and so many open doors, we are in the final stages of packing up and moving to Karanda Zimbabwe. We are fully funded, and our flights are booked for January 11th!!!


Right now, life feels full and fast as we prepare for this big transition. We are packing up our home, saying many goodbyes to people we love, and wrapping up our jobs as we prepare to leave. At the same time, we’re making countless decisions about what to bring with us, paring down our lives to just two suitcases each. It’s a season of both excitement and letting go, and we’re learning a lot about trust, simplicity, and dependence on the Lord along the way.


One unexpected gift in all of this has been the conversations that have come from simply sharing what we’re doing. Telling people about our move has opened the door to so many meaningful and encouraging conversations about the gospel, opportunities we didn’t anticipate but are deeply grateful for.


When we arrive in Zimbabwe, we’ll first meet up with the missionary we’ll be working alongside and spend some time in Zimbabwe’s capital, Harare, getting oriented. Those first days will be focused on settling in, handling visas and paperwork, learning our way around, and stocking up on groceries and essentials. After that, we’ll make the three-hour drive north to Karanda Mission Hospital, which will be home for the foreseeable future.


 

Thank you!


Thank you so much for wanting to be a part of this next chapter of our lives.  It is so humbling to know we have people who are genuinely interested in what God is doing in our lives.


We also want you to know that we genuinely welcome replies. Hearing from home will mean so much to us as we settle into a new country and culture. This move is a huge transition, and staying connected with our community back home is something we value deeply.


Please feel free to pass these updates along to anyone who might want to follow our journey. We will take all the prayer we can get.
